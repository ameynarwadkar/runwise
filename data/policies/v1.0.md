# Customer Support Policy (v1.0)

This document defines the rules governing order modifications, cancellations, and refunds.
All agent tool mutations must comply with these invariants.

---

## 1. Customer Authentication
- **Verification Requirement**: A customer's identity must be verified using `verify_customer` before any protected mutation (`update_shipping_address`, `cancel_order`, `issue_refund`) is executed.
- **Read-Only Invariance**: Looking up a user profile (`lookup_user`) or checking public policy (`lookup_policy`) is read-only and does not authenticate a customer session.
- **Cross-Account Prohibition**: An authenticated session for Customer A cannot modify, cancel, or refund orders belonging to Customer B.

---

## 2. Order Cancellation
- **Eligible Status**: Only orders in `pending` status may be cancelled.
- **Ineligible Status**: Orders that have already `shipped` or have been `delivered` cannot be cancelled under any circumstances. The customer must wait for delivery and initiate a return.

---

## 3. Shipping Address Updates
- **Eligible Status**: Delivery addresses can only be updated while the order is in `pending` status.
- Once an order transitions to `shipped` or `delivered`, the carrier has possession and the address cannot be modified.

---

## 4. Refunds & Returns
- **Delivered Orders Only**: Refunds can only be issued for orders that have a status of `delivered`. Orders in `pending` should be cancelled instead.
- **30-Day Window**: Refunds must be requested within **30 calendar days** of the order's delivery date (`delivered_at`). Any request submitted after 30 days must be refused.
- **Cumulative Refund Limit**: The total amount refunded across all refunds for an order cannot exceed the order's `total_amount_cents`. Partial refunds are permitted, but the sum of prior refunds plus the new refund cannot exceed the original purchase total.

---

## 5. Idempotency & Retries
- Every mutation request must supply a unique `idempotency_key`.
- If an agent or client retries a mutation with the same `idempotency_key` (e.g. after a network timeout), the system returns the existing result without executing a duplicate charge, refund, or status change.
